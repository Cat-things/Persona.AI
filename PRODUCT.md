# Product

## Register

product

## Users
People who enjoy long-running conversations and roleplay with AI characters, and the
homelab / VPS owners who host an instance for themselves, friends, or a small community.
Typical session: evening, on a phone in bed or a laptop on the couch, low ambient light,
20 to 90 minutes immersed in one or two conversations. Creators spend focused sessions
writing character definitions and testing them.

## Product Purpose
A self-hosted home for AI characters: discover or create characters, chat with streaming
replies, swipe between alternatives, talk to them by voice or call them. Success is a chat
that feels as smooth as the hosted apps people already know, running on hardware the owner
controls, with no data leaving it unless the admin chose a cloud provider.

## Brand Personality
Warm, unhurried, a little theatrical. Like a well-lit reading nook at night: the
characters are the stars, the interface is the stagehand. Voice is friendly and plain,
never cute, never corporate.

## Anti-references
- Character.AI's own identity (logo, wordmark, fonts, exact colors and assets): we share UX
  patterns, not looks.
- Neon "AI" aesthetics: purple-to-blue gradients, glowing orbs, glass cards.
- Generic SaaS dashboards: hero metrics, identical icon-card grids.
- Discord-style density in the chat view; messages need air.

## Design Principles
1. Characters first: avatars and names carry the color and personality; chrome stays quiet.
2. Familiar affordances: swipes, stars, sidebars behave the way users expect from the category.
3. Never wait silently: every generation, upload and connection shows live state.
4. Phone is a first-class surface, not a squeezed desktop.
5. Self-hosting is visible but never in the way: setup hints appear where something is missing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard support for chat actions (swipe with arrow keys when focused),
visible focus rings, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, color never the only state signal
(ratings use filled shapes + labels), screen-reader announcements for streamed replies once
complete.
